# Additional multi-round source audit — 2026-10-26 (seven passes)

## 1. Established proof-tier definitions

| Marker | Tier | Accepted criterion |
|---|---|---|
| `!` | B-Tier | On-paper derivation justified by reasoning; **not** yet a Lean-verified theorem |
| `!!` | A-Tier | **Exact stated** Lean theorem successfully elaborated/compiled and transitively axiom-audited, with no `sorryAx`, project-specific axioms or undisclosed premises |
| `!!!` | Formal closure | A-Tier conditional chain reduces the specified final result to a finite terminating witness checker, but no concrete witness has passed |
| `!!!!` | S-Tier | A finite raw-data witness **has actually passed** the checker and the proof from admission to the stated final conclusion is Lean-verified |

The build result for a **subclaim**, **corrected substitute**, **conditional implication** or **counterexample** never transfers the corresponding A-Tier marker to the still-unproved complete printed theorem. `cross-repo` is a provenance status, not a proof tier.
