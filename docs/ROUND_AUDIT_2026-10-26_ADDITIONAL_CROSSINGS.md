## 1. Established GOODWORKFLOWFRONTIER tiers

| Marker | Tier | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|
| `!` | B-Tier | On-paper derivation conditional on mathematical reasoning; no Lean certificate |
| `!!` | A-Tier | An **exact Lean statement**, compiled and transitively axiom-audited without sorryAx, custom axioms or unmentioned hypotheses |
| `!!!` | Formal closure | A-Tier conditional chain reduces final target to a terminating finite checker, with no accepted witness yet |
| `!!!!` | S-Tier | A concrete finite raw-data witness is accepted by that checker, with verified downstream transfer |

An exact theorem's `!!` marker must not be inflated to a larger or incorrect manuscript claim; a corrected theorem, a local counterexample, a generic finite identity and a conditional implication are different mathematical statements.
