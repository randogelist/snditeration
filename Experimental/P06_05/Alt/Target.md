## Selected alternative target

For an actual β(4) pencil from the calibrated 6.4 alternative and each admissible selected prime q, compute its integral scaled Lagrange matrix and prove that its reduction modulo q has nonzero determinant. Derive nonvanishing and the valuation from that verified reduction and explicitly tracked scaling factors; make no universal q≡1 mod8 valuation assertion in advance.

Relation to original: **restricted**.

Replace the universal printed valuation by a family of exact locally verified residue-matrix results.

## Remaining obligations

- Finish the actual β(4) pencil and its Lagrange identity.
- Prove harmonic residual integrality and compute diagonal/off-diagonal reductions.
- Give a uniform argument if claiming coverage for every prime in the printed congruence class.

Correction gate: The reduction must belong to the actual pencil. A selected-prime result or supplied nonzero determinant is not a proof of the all-prime target.
